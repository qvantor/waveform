# CLAUDE.md

This file provides guidance to Claude Code (claude.ai/code) when working with code in this repository.

## Project

Waveform is an experimental browser wavetable synth (TypeScript + Web Audio API, React 18). It is an Nx 14 monorepo with one app (`apps/waveform`) and four libs (`libs/math`, `libs/rxjs-react`, `libs/ui-kit`, `libs/preset-schema`), plus Netlify serverless functions in `netlify/` and a Postgres database for presets. Deployed to Netlify.

## Architecture

### State: `rxModel` (libs/rxjs-react)

All app state and audio logic live in RxJS "models", not React state. The pattern:

```ts
const model = (deps) =>
  rxModel((initial) => ({ $foo: new PrimitiveBS(0), audioNode, ... }))  // state: BehaviorSubjects + Web Audio nodes
    .actions((model) => ({ setFoo: (v) => model.$foo.next(v) }))
    .subscriptions((model, actions) => [ ...rx subscriptions wiring state to audio... ])
    .plugins(urlSnapshotPlugin({ modelToSnap, applySnap }))
    .destroy((model) => { ... });

export const { ModelProvider: FooProvider, useModel: useFoo } = rxModelReact('fooModel', model);
```

- Each builder call returns a new immutable factory; `init` runs actions, subscriptions, and plugin `onInit`; unmount runs `destroy`, unsubscribes, and plugin `onStop`.
- `rxModelReact` wraps a model in a React context. `<FooProvider initial={...} {...deps}>` takes the `initial` value plus dependency props (other models' `[model, actions]` tuples); `useFoo()` returns `[model, actions]`.
- Components read observables via `useBehaviorSubject($x)` / `useObservable`. BehaviorSubject helpers: `PrimitiveBS`, `ArrayBS`, `ObjectBS`. Observable names use a `$` prefix.
- Dependency injection is done through provider nesting, so provider order matters. The tree in `apps/waveform/src/app/synth/index.tsx` defines it: InputController → SynthCore → Envelopes → Modulation → Oscillator1/2 → MidiController → Filter → Synth (the filter in its voices) → Fx (follows the synth's last note) → SoundSwitch (a preset switch silences them) → KeyboardController → Presets.

### Audio graph (apps/waveform/src/app/synth/common/modules)

- `synth-core.ts` owns the single `AudioContext` and its wavetable oscillator (`wavetables`, `createWavetableEngine`): `voices node → preGain → fxIn → [fx chain] → fxOut → masterGain → masterLimiter → destination` (every note plays in the voices node, the filter is in each voice). `mute(seconds)`/`unmute()` (the sound switch) fade `fxOut.gain` out and step it back to 1, nothing else automates it; the limiter's `clear` (its `fx_clear`) forgets the gain reduction of the sound before. `$masterGain` is linear, the Master knob (`master-handle.tsx`) shows it in dB from −40 (silence) to +12: above 0 dB it drives the limiter. The header analysers tap `output` (the limiter's output).
- The latency of the output (`synth/common/latency/`): the `latencyHint` of the context, chosen at its creation (`resolveLatency` of `latency.ts`), so a change reloads the page (`reloadPage`: the tab keeps the sound and the MIDI input). Only Chromium on a desktop takes the hint (`canChooseLatency`: `navigator.userAgentData`, its brands, not `mobile`; Safari and Firefox ignore it, they get none): low is `0` (128 frames: `baseLatency` 2.67 ms + `outputLatency` 8 ms at 48 kHz on macOS), normal `'interactive'` (256 frames: 5.33 + 16 ms; a hint between them rounds up to 256). The mode (localStorage `latencyMode`: auto, low, normal; where the storage keeps nothing, site data blocked or full, `canKeepLatency`, the latency is normal and the settings offer no choice) is chosen under the Settings key, right of Midi (`settings/settings-button.tsx`; the page reloads, so its select has no ◀ ▶, `arrows` of the ui-kit `Select`); auto starts low and plays normal once it learnt it (localStorage `latencyAuto`; auto chosen again forgets it), only where the browser counts dropouts (`canCountDropouts` of `dropouts.ts`: `AudioPlaybackStats` with `underrunEvents`, the interface, not an instance, and `playbackStats` on `AudioContext.prototype`; Chromium before 146 has none: auto is normal there, nothing would warn of dropouts at low or leave it; a low chosen by hand stays, its note asks to choose normal if the sound crackles). At low the synth core polls the dropouts the browser counts (`playbackStats.underrunEvents`, `dropouts.ts`; Chrome 155 has it, Chromium 133 not: no guard there) once a second, `$dropouts` (the settings show them): `DROPOUT_SECONDS` (3) seconds with new ones within `DROPOUT_WINDOW` (60 s of the clock, `performance.now`: a hidden page polls once a minute) and auto keeps normal from the next start on (those while a preset switch silences the output, `$muted` of the synth core, and within `REPORT_LAG` 2 s after are shown, not counted), `$dropoutWarning` shows a toast that offers normal now (`switchToNormalLatency`: a low chosen by hand becomes normal; once, `dismissDropoutWarning`). The clocks of the context don't show a dropout (`currentTime`, `getOutputTimestamp`), and Chromium 133 grows its FIFO after one (its PushPullFIFO earmark) without telling `outputLatency`: a lateness worked out in a worklet (no `performance` there, `Date.now()` only) is not one.
- The master limiter (`synth/common/limiter/`) is the last stage, after the master volume. Its core (`dsp/limiter`, `limiter-wasm.ts`, the ceiling in `limiter-params.ts`; tested offline: `limiter-dsp.spec.ts`) is a stereo linked brickwall at −0.5 dBTP with a 4× true peak detector (`dsp-core/src/true_peak.rs`, polyphase Kaiser sinc whose phase 0 is the input itself), 2 ms lookahead, the needed gain held for 20 ms (the gain never moves inside a bass wave), a fast (50 ms) and a slow (0.5 s) release envelope (short peaks let go fast, dense parts slowly, no pumping between their notes; once the sound has needed less than the slow one for 250 ms it lets go at the fast rate, down to what the sound still needs) and two moving averages over the lookahead (S-shaped attack). The detector skips the interpolation while the last 32 samples are below the ceiling / `TRUE_PEAK_BOUND` (−8.6 dBFS: no point between them can reach the ceiling), and sums its 3 phases in one pass over the history, so quiet parts cost 0.2% of a core (about 0.6% while limiting; the TS core of before stage 5, the Rust one about half) and the output is the same to the bit. No sample goes over the ceiling, the true peak of band-limited sound stays under 0 dBFS (the 4× grid reads at most cos(π·f/4) low), below the ceiling, once released, the sound passes bit for bit, delayed by `latency` (2.3 ms at 48 kHz). `limiter.worklet.ts` registers the processor, `master-limiter.ts` loads it with `createWorkletFx`; until it is ready (while it loads, after the module or its processor failed) the master is silent (no fallback: the oscillators need the same, AudioWorklet and wasm SIMD), a failed one shows a toast on every screen (`LimiterStatus`, `synth/synth/components/`), its Retry loads it again, with the oscillators if they failed too (`retryAudio` of the synth core).
- The `fx` model (`synth/fx/`) wires the FX rack between `fxIn` and `fxOut`. Effects are defined in `FX_REGISTRY` (`fx/effects/`, params and ranges from `FX_PARAMS` in `libs/preset-schema`). `createFxSlot` wraps each one: `mix` is a crossfade (`mixGains`: 0 only dry, 1 only wet; equal power for delay and reverb, both at −3 dB at 0.5; linear for a core with `linearMix`, the distortion: its wet signal is the dry one reshaped and in time with it, they add up like one sound and equal power would be 3 dB louder in between; the default of a fresh effect is per type, `FX_MIX_PARAMS`: the distortion is all wet, delay and reverb 0.3), bypass passes only the dry signal at full level; a core with `latency` (the distortion) gets its dry signal delayed as much (`dryDelay`), so the timing never jumps and the mix doesn't comb filter. The chain is the added effects in `$order`; adding an effect type means a registry entry plus its preset schema. `clear()` (the sound switch, after the next preset is applied) makes every effect forget its tail at once, in the chain or not (`FxCore.clear`), and sets the gains of its slot (send, dry, wet) at its knobs at once (a mix its routes move follows them): the worklets post `{ clear }` (`fx_clear` of their cores: emptied and idle as once drained, the next sound starts at the params as they are then, no glide from those before; a reverb idle already is empty), the delay makes its lines, bandpasses and pan offsets again (a DelayNode can't be emptied: fed silence it plays its last second) at the values of its knobs at once (`instantly` of `createFxParams`: from the defaults of a node Time would chirp). `FX_TYPES` is the default chain order (distortion, delay, reverb); `normalizeFxOrder` puts a type missing from a saved order (saved before it existed) at its default place, before the first effect that follows it in `FX_TYPES`, not at the end.
- The reverb runs in an AudioWorklet: its core (`dsp/reverb`, `reverb-wasm.ts`, its params in `reverb-params.ts`) is a 16-line FDN (tested offline with impulse responses: `reverb-dsp.spec.ts`; the tables of its lines in `reverb-decay.ts` and `reverb-era.ts`, the chart draws them), `reverb.worklet.ts` registers the processor, `reverb.ts` creates the `AudioWorkletNode` once `loadWorklet` has added the module and posts param changes to it. The worklet modules register their processor with `registerStereoProcessor` (`fx/effects/stereo-processor.ts`: the AudioWorkletGlobalScope declarations, a mono input feeds both sides, a disconnected one is silence, `stop` ends it), their nodes are created with `STEREO_WORKLET_OPTIONS`. A worklet module must not import `@waveform/ui-kit`, `@waveform/preset-schema` (zod) or anything touching `window`/`document`. The synth core adds the limiter and the wavetable-osc modules itself, so specs that count `addModule` calls count only their module (`workletLoads` in `test-utils`). In a hot loop of JS (a worklet of the app, the probe of the perf build), don't chain calls per sample that return doubles through closures or polymorphic sites: V8 boxes every such result (a HeapNumber), so the audio thread makes garbage and burns CPU; run each stage over the block in typed arrays (the TS cores of before stage 5 did).
- The distortion runs in an AudioWorklet too (`distortion.ts`, `distortion.worklet.ts`): its core (`dsp/distortion`, `distortion-wasm.ts`, its params in `distortion-params.ts`; tested offline, aliasing included: `distortion-dsp.spec.ts`) drives the input (0 to 12 dB: more folds back audibly on high notes at 4×) into the curve of the mode 4× oversampled (the hard clip 8×, below) (`dsp-core/src/stereo.rs`: polyphase windowed sincs, `fir.sincPhase` in `libs/math`, linear phase, `DISTORTION_LATENCY` = 32 samples; the decimator is the one of the true peak detector, cut at the nyquist, the interpolator is cut at 0.9 × the nyquist, flat up to 0.4·fs: cut at the nyquist it passed half of the images of the partials just below it, which an oscillator plays, and the curve mixed them into inharmonic tones, so its phase 0 is not the input itself), × its makeup gain (`MAKEUP_TABLES` of `consts.rs`, worked out as `test-utils/distortion-makeup.ts` does: a table per mode over the drive that keeps the RMS of a reference sine of `REFERENCE_PEAK` 0.5, a note at the fx input; 1:1 with the drive while the reference is unclipped, then it levels off towards −9 dB, −7.1 dB soft and −8 dB hard at 12, so louder sounds come out quieter and quieter ones louder: the curve compresses), then the tone lowpass (at the top of its range, `TONE_OPEN`, it is faded out: the wet keeps the phase of the dry), the level (a trim on top of the makeup) and a DC blocker (one pole at 5 Hz, `DC_CUTOFF`: the curves are odd, but an asymmetric wave, a narrow pulse, gets DC from them, which would eat into the headroom of the limiter; the tests of the gains undo it, `unblock`); drive, makeup, level, tone and a new mode (a crossfade of the curves, each with its makeup; the mode changed back during a fade turns it around where it is) glide. The gains go on the oversampled points, before the decimator, so a crossfade never mixes points with and without them. The chart shows the curve of the mode at the drive within ±1, without the makeup and the level (they only scale the output). The modes are `DISTORTION_MODES` (`distortion-modes.ts`: soft clip = tanh, hard clip): the `mode` param is an index into it, so new modes are appended and `FX_PARAMS.distortion.mode.max` raised; a curve is odd, within ±1 and has the slope 1 at 0 (bitcrush would need a state of its own). It has no loops: `setActive` does nothing; once its input has been silent longer than the oversampler rings only the tails of the tone and the DC blocker are rendered (about 0.7 s after a DC-heavy sound, at 0.15% of a core), then the worklet clears its state and skips the blocks. Each stage runs over the block (`up`/`down` of the oversamplers and the `shape_block` of the mode, both channels in the lanes of f64x2), the output is the same to the bit as sample by sample. The TS cores of before stage 5 cost about 1% of a core while playing (soft clip 1.1%, hard clip 0.95%, reverb 2.6%), the Rust ones half (`dsp.mjs`), 0.05% unused.
- A worklet effect builds on `createWorkletFx` (`fx/effects/worklet-fx.ts`: loading, input → node → output, `$status`, `retry`, `destroy` posts `stop` to the processor, so does a failure: a processor that threw still takes it, its core is freed; a stopped processor takes no message). Without AudioWorklet (http on a LAN IP, old Safari) or when its module fails, the app still works: the fx model keeps the effect out of the chain (it stays `added` in the snapshot), and the Effects list shows why under its name. An unsupported effect can't be added, an added one that can't be used can still be removed, a failed one loads again on Retry or when it is added. Until an effect is `ready` (also while it loads) its slot passes only the dry signal and is inactive.
- The oscillators run in an AudioWorklet (`synth/common/wavetable/`), there is no native fallback: without AudioWorklet, Worker or MessageChannel the synth doesn't play and each oscillator shows why over its wave (`OscillatorStatus`, Retry for a module that failed: `retryAudio`, the limiter too if it failed); else why its table doesn't: the store refused it or the worker failed to build it (the previous one plays, Retry sends it again). `wavetable-engine.ts` (one per context, in the synth core): `$status` loading/ready/unsupported/failed (the module, the worker, the store or the voices node) and `retry` (as `createWorkletFx`), the store node (`STORE_PROCESSOR`, 0 inputs, 1 output never connected: 0/0 is not allowed), the voices node (`VOICES_PROCESSOR`, into `preGain`: every note), the worker (`wavetable.worker.ts` → `wavetable-builder.ts`) and a MessageChannel between them: `setTable(id, frames)` copies the frames into Float32Arrays transferred to the worker, the worker builds the levels (`wavetable-mipmap.ts`, a newer version of a table replaces a waiting one) and transfers them to the store; the store (`createTableStoreWasm`, one per AudioWorkletGlobalScope, refcounted versions in the memory of the core) answers `{stored, version}` or `{refused, version}` (no memory for it) and `{freedBytes}` once it frees versions. The engine hears the store and the worker in every build (the port started: nothing piles up in it): `$tables` gives each table `sending`, `stored`, `refused` or `failed` (the worker's `{failed, version, reason}`: a build that threw, an allocation of a phone; only an error of the worker itself fails the engine) for its latest version, `retryTable(id)` sends it again, a refused version goes again once when the store frees memory (refused again, it waits for Retry); the oscillator model has its own (`$tableState`, `retryTable`). Tables are kept and sent again after a retry, versions only grow. `createVoice(options, values)` makes the voice of a note in the voices node, null until ready (a note played meanwhile isn't played later): a `VoiceHandle`, its id and `params`, per param the AudioParam calls the synth makes (`ParamAutomation`: setValueAtTime, setTargetAtTime, exponentialRampToValueAtTime, cancelScheduledValues), each an event of the node (its value in f32, as an AudioParam keeps it; none once the voice has ended); `setRoutes(ids, routes)`, `setFilter(ids, filter)` one event for the voices that get the same; the events of a task go in one message (`{ events }`, a microtask sends them; `flush()` the specs), in the order they were made. The node posts after a block that has any `{ started: [[id, started, scheduled]], ended: [id] }` (the handle's `onStarted`, `onEnded`); an error of the voices node (`onprocessorerror`) fails the engine: it is stopped (`{ stop: true }`: its voices give their tables back), every voice ends (the synth lets their keys go), Retry makes it again; destroy stops it, telling none. For the perf suite and the specs (the perf build and Jest, under `__PERF__`): `pending()` (tables whose latest version isn't in the store yet) and `stats()` asks the store (`{stats: true}`: versions and bytes held, voices alive and their unison, samples played from mix tables or directly, mix builds and refusals, mix tables allocated and their bytes, counted by the shared of the core, `createVoiceSharedWasm`) and the worker (pending tables, freed-buffer pool, build ms), with the voices of the voices node and the events their timelines hold (`scopeVoices`, `events`); a voice with `trace` in its options is told started (the frames) once, the synth traces voices only while its `$voiceStarts` is observed (every voice that started late is told too, production included: the fx and Poly follow it; so is every voice of mono, on time too, `mono` of its options: the synth keeps its starts again until then).
- Levels (`wavetable-mipmap.ts`, `wavetable-levels.ts` has the layout without imports, so the worklet bundles no FFT): a third of an octave apart on the grid of Chrome's PeriodicWave (`levelBase`: harmonics up to 1024 · 2^(−r/3), at most the frame's 1023, then a silent level of 1 sample), each 32 samples per cycle of its highest harmonic long, 512..4096 (`levelLength`), 1 guard sample before a frame and 2 after it; DC and the nyquist bin dropped, a frame at the volume of the file (not normalized, as createPeriodicWave does; level B of the plan), phases kept, frames of 16..2048 samples, `MAX_FRAMES` 256 (≈ 63.6 MiB). Building 256 frames takes ~150 ms (Firefox ~300).
- The voice (the core `dsp/wavetable-osc`: `voice.rs`, through `wavetable-osc-wasm.ts`, its options and messages in `voice-options.ts`; tested offline: `wavetable-osc-dsp.spec.ts`; `wavetable-osc.worklet.ts` registers the voices processor and the store): every note is a voice of the voices node (`VOICES_PROCESSOR`, one per context; `voices-processor.ts`, its core `createVoiceScopeWasm`, `scope.rs`), both oscillators (null in `options.osc`: off for the note) with their unison voices; the node renders its voices in the order they were made by the same `Voice::process` and sums them in f32 in that order into one stereo output. A voice comes as an `on` event (its options and the values of its params at the note on) and its params as automation events, each param of each voice a timeline of the core (`timeline.rs`) with the semantics of an AudioParam: setValueAtTime, setTargetAtTime, exponentialRampToValueAtTime, linearRampToValueAtTime (bench-osc's LFOs; the synth makes none), cancelScheduledValues, by the formulas of the Web Audio spec in f64 from values in f32, each value in f32 clamped to the range of its descriptor (`VOICE_PARAMETERS`); an event acts from the first frame not before its time, one that comes later (a message comes between the quanta, automation came synchronously: a release or a knob may be a quantum later than it was) from the block it comes in, one of the same kind at the same time replaces the one there (as Chromium), the events of a voice that has ended are nothing, those the history of 0.5 s has passed are forgotten (their value kept); a block without a change is one value (as a browser gives a param without automation). The browsers worked their curves out with code of their own: after a glide or a knob turned a voice differs in the last bits of f32 from what a node per note played (a frequency or a detune one ulp apart moves the phase), which nothing hears and no golden sees. Params (`wavetable-osc-params.ts`): `frequency` (the note, portamento glides it), `gate` (1 the key is held, `RELEASE_GATE` + h: released after h seconds, 0: released at that sample, below 0 fades a stolen voice out in 20 ms, a raised cosine; held at another value (1 and 1.5 in turn) or held again after a release: the envelopes start again at that sample (mono), each from the level it has there (`retrigger` of `envelope.rs`: the samples of the block before it keep the envelope they had, a release is off), not on a stolen voice nor past its end; an automation event, exact to the sample from its time on), per oscillator `position` (share of the table, frame k of N at k/(N−1)), `detune` (cents: 1200·octave), `spread` (cents over the unison), `gain`. Its options (what is read at the note on): the table id, the unison (1..8, one voice at spread 0 unless a route raises the spread, a stack or a position spread keep them), `phase`/`phaseRand` (shares of a period), `unisonShape` (below), `envelopes` (ENV 1–3) and `envStart` (in legato the start of the phrase), `routes` and `filter`. The volume is ENV 1 (`envelope.rs`, exact to the sample, the release from the level reached); the voice ends where ENV 1 is back at 0 and the node tells it ended (it leaves the node in that block). A voice processed after its start (the render lag: its note on came a device buffer late) starts its envelopes at its first sample (`startAt`), the attack is not skipped; a note of a legato phrase starts them where the first voice of the phrase did (`join_phrase`/`phrase_frame` of the shared, `shared.rs`: the phrases of the scope by the frame they are scheduled at, each kept while a voice of it plays, so a note out of legato in between doesn't hide one; its first voice was created before, if it hasn't started it starts in the same block), so the voices of a phrase stay in step. The release starts h after the first sample of the voice (or its last start again; the key held h, the note on and the gate each reach the audio thread with a lag of its own), not before the sample the gate is seen at, at most `RELEASE_LEAD` after it. Routes (`modulation.rs`) replace the params they move by base + Σ amount × level (base × 2^Σ for a log one), clamped to the knob's range; while every envelope a param follows is in a flat stage and its amounts don't glide it is one value (the voice stays on its mix table), an envelope no route reads is not rendered, a changed route glides in 10 ms (a `routes` event). The filter (`filter.rs`, a `filter` event, null: off) runs after the oscillators and before ENV 1, coefficients of `coefficientsInto` (`@waveform/math/biquad`, an alias that keeps the FFT out of the bundle) worked out every 32 samples only when a value changed, its knobs glide ~10 ms (another type takes them at once, its state goes on: the Q of a bandpass is linear, of a lowpass in dB); a moved param is not copied: its values go into an override the voice reads instead of the param (`Osc.moved` per oscillator, `moved` of the filter, through the `ParamAccess` of each target; by a computed name V8's accesses go megamorphic). A node per note had 10 AudioParams that cost every block even without automation (~1.5% of a held note in Chrome each), its own port and messages, and the browser summed the nodes: one node has none of it (values read once are options all the same). bench-perf `--against=main --headless` (5 runs a side, an output of 44.1 kHz: both resampled): the audio thread of S5 61.9 Mcps against 98.0 in Chromium (−37%, every run under every run of the node per note), L1 / L3 to the voice p50 0.32 / 0.28 ms against 0.42 / 0.37 (−24%; L1 p95 0.86 against 1.98), to the output and the memory the same. On an iPhone 15 Pro (iOS 26.5.2, Safari through safaridriver over HTTPS on the LAN, the Time Profiler of the device, 2 runs a side of S5 held 12 s, its chords every 1.2 s, then 2 × 30 every 300 ms with stealing): the longest stretch the audio thread ran without a break 4–5 ms against 8–9 ms with a node per note, none of 6 ms or more against 3–11 in a phase of fast chords; the port messages under 1% of its samples against 6–15% there, the lock waits 1–6% against 11–24%; the clock of the context fell behind none of the 0.1 s steps against 1–2. Unison voice j is detuned by spread·(j/(n−1) − ½); the middle one of an odd count both channels, the others alternately right and left from the lowest (`dsp/measurements/unison.json`: a voice and its mirror on opposite sides, the channels mirror each other, their sum is centred on the note; until the stacks the lowest was on the left, a mirror of the sound); each at 1/√⌈n/2⌉. The shape of the unison (`UnisonShape` of `voice-options.ts`, `shape_unison` of `voice.rs`; the defaults `UNISON_SHAPE` play as before it, bit for bit, the goldens hold it): `blend` the gain of the centre voices (pair 0: the middle one of an odd count, the middle two of an even one) against the others, c = min(1, 2(1−b)), s = min(1, 2b), both × √((1 + m)/(c² + m·s²)) with m the other voices per channel (the power of the unison stays; 0.5 all at 1: the renders play about one level at 50%, not at 75% as the plan had it; a unison of 2 is all centre); `width` (1 hard left and right; below it the oscillator renders into its own channels and each side channel takes the other × sin, itself × cos of (1 − w)·π/4: equal power, mono at 0; the centre stays); `stack` (`UNISON_STACKS`: 12 (1x, 2x, 3x), 12+7 (1x, 2x, 3x), Center−12/−24; voice j of n at d = j − ⌊n/2⌋, the centre (d 0, and −1 of an even count) moves only by the centre stacks, a single voice too, the others by a = |d|: 12 (kx) up 12·(a mod (k + 1)), 12+7 (kx) up 7 at a mod 4 = 2 plus 12 ((k − 1)x); of an even count a voice and its mirror move apart; all 9 × 1..8 voices against the renders); `detune` (`UNISON_DETUNES`: of x = 2j/(n − 1) − 1, linear x (as before them, to the bit), super tan(x)/tan(1), exp x·|x|, inv sin(x·π/2), against the renders of 3..16 voices within 0.3 cent; `random`: the shares of the spread at random, sorted from the lowest, drawn by the facade after the 8 phases of the oscillator, only then: the others keep the draws of before); `positionSpread` (the voices at the position + its share × their share of the spread, within the table, the moving position too; a mix per voice as before). The detune mode is no parameter of the Audio Unit of the stand: it sets `kParamDetuneMode` in the plain params of its osc A (its state, `scripts/renders/state.mjs`). Its cost (`voice u8 shaped unison` of `dsp.mjs`, a u8 at a blend, a width of 0.5, a stack and a position spread): 12.4 against 11.0 ns/sample of a plain u8 in Node, all of it the width (the channels of the oscillator and their pan: `mix_osc`, over slices, the gain once per loop; indexed per sample it cost twice as much, the path of a gain a route moves is the same loop), the rest ~1%. The oscillator model holds it (`$unisonShape`, `setUnisonShape`: no knobs and not in the snapshot or the schema yet; the perf hook reaches it through its models). Every voice starts at `phase` plus a random share of the period up to `phaseRand` (0: all in phase, their beats sweep through the spectrum and n voices are louder at the onset; 1: any phase, steady, the power of one voice); at `phaseRand` > 0 the voices start at once (the click on a fast attack is part of the sound; level B of the plan: the TS core faded them in over 1.5 ms); a note of a legato phrase fades in (it would step to the level of the phrase). Pitch → level as Chrome does (`1 + 3·log2(base·f/nyquist)`: floor(x) crossfaded with the next one), a position plays one frame, never a mix of two (`dsp/measurements/frames.json`; level B of the plan: the TS cores morphed frames k and k + 1): the share v of the table plays frame ceil(v·M) − 1 of its M frames (v·M in (k, k + 1] is frame k; a moving position steps to the next frame at the first sample past it); a changing pitch or position is worked out every 32 samples and interpolated. Levels are read linearly; while pitch, position and table stay (from the second block) each unison voice sums the frame × 2 levels it reads into a mix table as long as the native table (4096, 16384 above 88.2 kHz; shorter levels 6-point Lagrange interpolated into it once), built under a per-block budget (360k multiply-adds across the scope's voices, `MIX_BUDGET_WASM`: the core builds a mix twice as fast as the TS core did with its 200k, 360k is the most whose longest block of the attack of a chord of 12 × 2 × u8 is as long as the TS's, 0.170 against 0.172 ms in Node, its 192 mixes from block 26 instead of 56, 44% fewer samples played directly; level B of the plan), buffers pooled: a held note costs ~0.8× a native oscillator in Chrome with native aliasing. A param is constant when its array has one value or all equal (a silent input still gives 128; Firefox gives 128 equal values for a param without automation): the routes on a flat stage, the gate and the pitch take it as one value. A new version of a table is crossfaded into over 10 ms. Shared block buffers for all voices of the scope; Float32 channel buffers copied once into the outputs (writing the outputs many times was slow in WebKit).
- `oscillator.ts` holds the frames (`$waveTable`), `$morph` (the table plays expanded to 256 frames by crossfades: its file has a morph mark, or the audio-file editor made it; `setWaveTable(frames, morph)`, the engine sends it with the frames, the worker expands them: `wavetable-frames.ts`), `$frames` (the frames played: 256 expanded, else those of the table), the Wave position `$position` (in the frames played, 0..$frames−1; the knob steps by whole frames, a fractional one from a preset or a modulation plays the frame of its share; the editors work on `frameIndex(position, count, morph)` = the nearest frame of the table, of an expanded one among its 256; choosing one sets the position where it plays alone, `framePosition`), `$playedWave` (the chart: the frame the position plays, of an expanded table the crossfade of two of its frames; one period edge to edge, phase 0 to 360°), `$osc` (unison, detune, octave, phase, phaseRand), `$unisonShape` (the shape of its unison, read at the note on; not saved) and `$gain`; the Wave frame, Phase, Phase rand and Unison are shown in a strip under the chart, on its background (`oscillator/components/wave/wave-info.tsx`, `DragValue` of ui-kit: pressed and dragged, right or up raises it, Phase goes around, Unison with `arrows`: the carets of `DraggableNumber`, as Oct; the strip never widens the oscillator, `contain: inline-size`, below 380 px it is `compact`: less padding, 10 px text), while Phase or Phase rand is held the chart shows the phase on the wave and the band [phase, phase + rand) the voices start in, wrapped as they do (`phase-overlay.tsx`); `$params` is the base of the voice params from them. It sends its table to the engine under its `id` (a new table at once, edits of its frames at most every `TABLE_THROTTLE` 100 ms), removes it on destroy. It has no mod buses (the voices have no AudioParams): a global source (an LFO) would be a source in the core, as the envelopes are.
- `synth.ts` handles voices: a note gets a voice of the voices node (`createVoiceHandle`, `wavetables.createVoice`), with the envelopes, the routes (`voiceRoutes`: the osc knobs of the oscillators on for it, the filter knobs in the range of its type while it is on, amount 0 left out; FX targets go to the fx model) and the filter of its note on. Playing notes, released ones too, follow Gain, Detune and Wave (`setTargetAtTime` ~10 ms, then set exactly so the param is constant again) and Oct (at once), the routes and the filter (messages, only to the notes whose own routes changed); unison, phases, on/off and the envelopes only new notes. A key released sets the gate to `RELEASE_GATE` + the seconds it was held (`performance.now()`), now: the voice is released that long after its first sample. currentTime moves by device buffers (10 ms on Windows, up to 40 ms on Android), a note shorter than one released at currentTime would be released at its start, silent. The fx envelope is released as long after its start on the context clock, not before now and at most `RELEASE_LEAD` (50 ms) after it (a longer lead is a stalled clock; `releaseStart`, shared with the processor). A voice processed late posts the frame it started at: from then on the fx envelope starts there (the first voice of a legato phrase moves the phrase's), the release of the voice and the end of the release Poly counts are worked out from it, a release already sent moves with it; the starts again (mono) made before it are worked out again from there (`Voice.startLate`: those before its first sample are its start, the voice sees the gate there). Poly counts the released voices until they end (or their release is over; released where ENV 1 is at 0 it ends there, as the voice does): above it (all those above it at once, after Poly went down) the quietest released voice is stolen first (its ENV 1 now, `Voice.level`; the oldest of equal ones: the smallest step), then the oldest held one. Portamento (`glide.ts`): a note pressed while another key is held glides from it, in legato too, none at 0; convex in pitch (p₀ + (p₁ − p₀)·(1 − (1 − t/T)²)), there exactly after its time, in 8 `exponentialRampToValueAtTime` of `frequency` (no `setValueCurveAtTime`). Legato (Poly > 1): the notes pressed while another one is held start with the envelopes and the start of the phrase (they don't retrigger), each releases at its own key. Mono (Poly 1, `Voicing.mono`: one voice and the stack of the keys held, the last pressed on top): a note while the voice sounds takes it over (the key it is held under moves; no new node, its phases, filter and mix tables go on). Over a held key it glides there from where its pitch is (a glide it interrupts is held at the point of its own line, `glideFrom`: not `cancelAndHoldAtTime`), in legato its envelopes go on, else they start again (`Voice.retrigger`: the gate held at its other value now, each envelope from the level it has reached; two notes at one currentTime keep the value, the second replaces the first before it is seen); from its release (no key held) the pitch steps (Always is off) and they start again. The key on top released hands the voice back to the key below as if it were pressed, a key below released changes nothing, the last key releases it (h from its last start again). A new voice instead (the old one stolen) when its release ends within `RELEASE_LEAD` (the voice would end before it sees the gate) or when it would start envelopes other than the knobs' or play other oscillators (`Voice.playsKnobs`: on/off, the unison and its shape; not the phases, they go on) (the voice reads them at its note on; a glide in legato keeps it). A voice of mono has a phrase of its own. Poly coming to 1 or leaving it while notes play: the voice plays on as a voice of its note (its other keys release nothing), the next note follows the new mode; a voice of mono that ended by itself: the next note starts one. `$fxNote` is the envelopes of the last note pressed (in legato of the phrase, released with its last key; in mono a start again is a new note from then, `from`: the levels its envelopes start their attacks from), the fx follow it. Every voice stays `sounding` (it follows the knobs, a preset switch waits for it) until the voices node tells it `ended`; the events of a voice that has ended are not sent. A voice that traps in the node ends the same way, alone: the facade of the core catches the trap (the stack pointer put back), the voice gives its tables back to the store, it is told ended and its output of the block is not in the sum (the stores of the scope before it are in the memory then: a `compiler_fence` before each voice), the others render on, the engine stays ready; a note on that traps is told ended too, so is a voice an event finds no memory for (its timeline can't grow: a release lost would hold its note); a call of a message that traps (the routes or the filter of a voice, a batch of events) loses only what it carried, the events after it are applied (`guard` of `apply`); an event of a param the core has not is nothing. An error of the voices node itself fails the engine (every voice ends, Retry). A held voice that ended so frees its key: Poly doesn't count it, no glide starts from it, the fx release their envelopes then, as at a key's release (`letGo`). The pitch bend is the voices node's (`setBend` of the engine, one `bend` event for every voice: `bendCents`, 100 · its share × the range down or up, `v.bd`/`v.bu` semitones 0..48, 2 by default; at most every 5 ms, `BEND_INTERVAL`, `throttleTime`: the first value at once, the last after the pause): the scope glides to it in 10 ms (`Glided` of `glided.rs`) and puts it on the detune of both oscillators of every voice, released ones and new ones too (`bend_into` of `scope.rs`; at rest nothing: the sound of before, to the bit), a node made again (Retry) takes it at once; the detune of a voice stays its octave (an octave steps at once, under the bend); while it moves the voices play directly, then on their mix tables again.
- A preset switch silences the synth (`sound-switch.ts`, the `soundSwitch` model: the synth core, the synth, the fx and both oscillators; `switchSound(apply)`): no mix of two presets (the voices and the tails of the one before with the knobs of the next one, its knobs on the tables of the one before). The output fades out (synth core `mute`) in `KILL_FADE` (20 ms, the fade of a stolen voice) and the synth pauses (`pause`: every voice fades out, the fx follow no note; the keys held, in mono its stack, and those pressed meanwhile wait, none makes a voice, a key released leaves them); once `currentTime` is past the fade and the voices it faded out have ended (the voices node sees the gate a block or a device buffer after the main thread set it) (polled every 10 ms; a context that isn't running isn't waited for, a clock that doesn't move 200 ms at most, `MAX_CLOCK_WAIT`, after the preset too) the preset is applied, so the chain rewired then doesn't click, then the fx and the limiter forget the sound before (`clear`; after it: the delay makes its nodes at the knobs of the preset, from those before Time would glide); one chosen meanwhile replaces it. The output steps back to 1 (`unmute`: nothing sounds before it, a fade would take the attacks of the keys) `SWITCH_LEAD` (10 ms: the clear messages reach the worklets first) after it, once the oscillators on play the tables of their paths (`tablePlayed` of the oscillator model: not loading nor `sending`; refused or failed: the version before plays), 2 s at most (`MAX_SILENCE`); a preset that throws as it is applied doesn't keep the synth silent (its error goes on: to the caller of `switchSound`, uncaught from the poll; the fx and the limiter are cleared all the same, the output opens on what it set); then the keys still held start on it (`resume`: in their order, no glide between them, in its mode: in mono the last one, the others under it in its stack). A preset chosen while silent is applied at once and the wait starts again.
- Envelopes (`synth/common/envelope/`): `envelope-curve.ts` (no imports, shared by the worklets, the main thread and the specs) is an envelope as a function of time, the release from the level reached to 0 in its time; its attack, decay and release are linear, or curved by `curves` (k of `curveShape`: (1 − e^(−k·x)) / (1 − e^(−k)) over the share x of the stage; a linear stage is worked out as before, to the bit). The curves are in the preset (`curves: { attack, decay, release }` of each envelope, k within ±`ENVELOPE_CURVE_MAX` 10), a fresh envelope has the default shape, `ENVELOPE_CURVES` of the schema (a linear attack, the decay and the release at k 3.49: the renders of `dsp/measurements/envelopes.json`, their curves 25/50/66.6/75% are k −5.41/0/3.49/5.41 within 1%; level B of the plan; a preset saved before them gets it); a note takes them with the knobs at its note on (`synth.ts`: the voices, the fx note, the marks; mono starts a new voice for other curves, as for other times). The chart draws the curve (`envelopeLine` of ui-kit, `envelope-line.ts`: `CURVE_POINTS` a curved stage, the squares at the corners; the dot has 16 points on a curved stage of it, `noteCorners`) and has a point on each stage longer than 20 ms (`CURVE_HANDLE_MIN_TIME`) that moves the level (none on a decay to a sustain of 1 or a release from 0: `CurveHandles` of ui-kit, `curve-handles.tsx`): in the middle of the stage on its curve, where the share of the stage done is 1 / (1 + e^(−k/2)); dragged up or down it follows the pointer (`curveFromMidShare`: k = 2·ln(s / (1 − s)), steps of 0.01), within 3 px of the line it snaps to k 0, ↑/↓ move it, a double click gives the stage its default curve again. The voice renders a curved stage by a recurrence (e^(−k·x) times a factor a sample, no exp per sample: `envelope.rs`); the fx automation cuts a curved stage into 15 linear segments (`curvedAt`); the voice modulation reads the envelopes every 32 samples as before (a chord of a curve); started again on a voice that sounds (mono) its attack goes from the level reached (`from`) to 1 in its time, its stages end where those of a new note do; `envelope.rs` of the core renders one per block in the voice (a block within a flat stage is one value). The `env` model (`envelopes.ts`, snapshot `env: { e1, e2, e3 }`, stages 0..32 s, `ENVELOPE_DEFAULTS` in the schema, the curves of each, `setEnvelopeCurve`) holds ENV 1–3, `$selected` (the tab, UI state) is not saved; the time knobs are `mode: 'pow'` (short times get most of the travel). The chart of the open tab has a dot of the last note pressed (`EnvelopeDot`, `envelope/`: `$fxNote`, as the fx and the marks follow it; ENV 1 always, ENV 2 and 3 while a route reads them): its path (`noteCorners` of `note-path.ts`) is always on the line of the chart: the knobs it draws (a knob turned while the note plays moves the dot with the line), released before the sustain a step to the sustain corner, then along the release line; a note that started the envelope again starts on the attack line at its level; it is one Web Animation of transform and opacity of an HTML element (`NoteDot` of ui-kit, over the chart: the `overlay` of `LineChart`), started where the output plays the start of the note (`outputTime`: `getOutputTimestamp`, else the latencies of the context); the compositor moves it, no frame runs on the main thread, on the sustain it is finished, after the release hidden (fill none), a knob turned after that (a longer release) doesn't bring it back; it is made again only on a note on or off, another tab or new scales.
- Modulation: the `modulation` model (`modulation.ts`, snapshot `mod: { 'env2:osc1.wave': 0.5 }`, one route per source and knob, at most `MOD_ROUTES_MAX` 64 in all and `MOD_KNOB_SOURCES_MAX` 4 on a knob: past them the knobs, or the full one, take no ENV tab and don't light up) holds the routes, amount −1..1 a share of the knob's range in its scale (octaves for a log knob, `amountInUnits`); `modulation-targets.ts` is the registry of the assignable knobs (`osc1.gain|wave|detune`, `filter.cutoff|resonance|gain` in the range of the filter type, `<fx>.<param>` and `<fx>.mix` for every knob of `FX_REGISTRY`, not the switches). The ENV tabs (`envelope-tabs.tsx`, a segmented control in the section header, a third of it each: `Section plain` has no header padding, the tabs are on its grey, as high as the other headers; ENV 1–3 green, blue, purple of the same lightness (OKLCH 0.65, chroma 0.12), `ENVELOPE_COLORS`; a dot of its color in a white ring before the white label of a tab, the selected one a white key with a dark label and a grip in its right corner; no focus outline) are dragged onto knobs with `startModDrag` (ui-kit, pointer events): a drop adds a route at +50% and selects the tab; `useKnobModulation(target)` gives a knob its `modTarget` and `modulations` (all its routes in the order of the sources, `selected` the one of the selected ENV tab, `$selected` of the env model: only it shows its arc over the ring of the knob, the dot at the top left shows all of them and changes it (drag it to change the amount); every route moves the live mark, where the routes move the knob in the last note while voices sound; only a knob with routes renders, when the first voice starts and the last one ends); the charts of the knobs follow it too: the wave (`osc1.wave`: the frame played where the routes move the position, the phase band a `<use>` of its path, the phase dot moved onto the wave drawn), the filter response (cutoff, resonance, gain in the range of the type), the curve of the distortion (Drive), the decay of the reverb (Size, Decay, Hi damp, Bass) and the bandpass of the delay (Freq, Q) are drawn by `useLiveDraw`, nothing while their section is off).
- FX modulation (`fx/effects/fx-modulation.ts`, `fx-automation.ts`): the fx model gives each effect `{ note: $fxNote, routes }` (the envelopes from `note.from` once a note started them again in mono) while it is in the chain, on and ready (null otherwise: nothing computed). The distortion and the reverb compute the moved knobs once per block in their worklets (`registerStereoProcessor`'s `modulate`: the modulator holds back the posted values of moved knobs, sets a value only when it changed; the reverb's Size and Predelay then glide in 10 ms, without their tape glide, its lines follow a moved Size); the delay's params and every slot's mix are automated (`scheduleRouted`: from 10 ms after now (`LEAD`: its events reach the audio thread before it renders there; Chrome's may render a quantum between two calls, Firefox renders a device buffer ahead of currentTime), from where the param is then (a hold: `fx-automation.ts` keeps what it scheduled on each param, `setParam`, `glideParam` (`setSmooth`) and its ramps, and holds at a point of that line, then cancels what follows; never `cancelAndHoldAtTime`: Chrome worked its value out on the audio thread, and from 0 when the audio thread got to the hold before the next events, or over a `setTargetAtTime`, a click of the whole mix) to the envelope in 10 ms, or by its first corner if it comes sooner (a short attack isn't skipped), a new note doesn't step, then ramps between the envelope's corners (worked out from the start of the note, the sums of its stages: exactly where a stage ends; a stage goes to its end, `routedUnclamped(…, before)`; a ramp only while it stays within the range, its ends may be on its ends, an envelope that steps there, an attack, a decay or a release of 0, steps the param), exponential for log knobs, 15 linear segments for clamped stages, the pan split at 0 and the equal power mix (no `setValueCurveAtTime`: an event inside a curve still running throws); Time without its tape glide; a delay mode change or an inactive delay leaves the automation of every feedback gain; the mix leaves its automation once when its routes go, its ticks without routes glide). The osc/fx switch (`TapeSwitch switchOnDrag`) flips when a dragged label stays on it 600 ms.
- `input-controller.ts` is the shared note-on/off bus, `$bend` the pitch bend as a share of its range (−1..1, 0 at rest; the last of the MIDI input and the wheel wins). The on-screen piano, `keyboard-controller.ts` (QWERTY) and `midi-controller.ts` (WebMIDI) all feed into it. The MIDI controller passes the pitch bend of its channel (`0xE0`, `midiBend`: 14 bits, 8192 at rest, 8191 steps up and 8192 down), and puts the bend it set back at rest where it releases its held notes (another channel, another input or none; the bend of the wheel stays). The wheel (`synth/pitch-bend/`, `PitchWheel` of ui-kit: left of the keys, its range Up/Down beside it, the keys narrower to fit; on a phone alone, in the lower tier) springs back to 0 when let go, on blur and on a lost pointerup. The MIDI input and channel the user chose are kept by the tab (sessionStorage `midiInput`, `readStored`/`writeStored` of `app/common/services`): the chosen input is selected whenever it is connected, meanwhile the selected one while it is, else the first one (selected for the user, not kept).

### Rust cores of the worklets (dsp/)

- The DSP of the worklet modules is Rust → wasm32, one `.wasm` per worklet bundle (`dsp/<crate>`, a cdylib with a C ABI, no wasm-bindgen and no SharedArrayBuffer), ported bit for bit from the TS cores, which were removed at stage 5 of the plan (two weeks in production without a regression; the goldens hold the sound since): a bundle `<name>.js` (`<name>.worklet.ts`) registers its processors (names in `common/processors.ts`) with their AudioParams and messages, its facade (`<name>-wasm.ts`) wraps the core, the options and messages are in `limiter-params.ts`, `reverb-params.ts`, `distortion-params.ts`, `wavetable/voice-options.ts`. A module with a core needs wasm SIMD (`isWorkletSupported(ctx, name)`, `WASM_MODULES` of `common/wasm/wasm-support.ts`: `unsupported` without it: the limiter and the oscillators are silent). The cores: the limiter (`dsp/limiter`), the reverb (`dsp/reverb`), the distortion (`dsp/distortion`), the wavetable oscillator with its store (`dsp/wavetable-osc`).
- The crates: `jsmath` (the Math of JS bit for bit: V8's fdlibm of `deps/v8/src/base/ieee754.cc` for sin, cos, exp, expm1, tanh, log, log2, log10, glibc's pow without FMA, as V8 calls std::pow; `round`, `max`, `min`, `to_int32` with the JS semantics; `jsmath-check` exports it to the specs), `dsp-core` (biquad coefficients and TDF2, the blocks of the effects, true peak, oversampler, the stereo io; `consts.rs`: the tables the TS cores worked out when their modules loaded, as the bits of each double, now its source), `simd.rs` (`F64x2`: two independent lanes, the operators of f64 and the `min`/`max` of wasm, which are those of Math) and `stereo.rs` (biquad, DC blocker and oversampler of both channels in the lanes: the distortion renders L and R at once), one crate per core. On wasm32 the crates are `no_std` (`wasm-rt`: a panic traps, dlmalloc as std uses it; `jsmath::floor/ceil/trunc/sqrt` are the wasm instructions), natively std (`cargo test`). `dsp/rust-toolchain.toml` pins the toolchain, `.cargo/config.toml` builds with `+simd128`, a 64 KiB stack and `__stack_pointer` exported (a trap leaves it moved, about 70 failed note ons used up the stack: every call of a core puts it back, `instantiateCore` wraps the exports, the calls of a block guard it where they are made, `fx_buffers` and `fx_process` in `stereo-core.ts`, `osc_io` and `osc_voice_process` in the voice, `osc_scope_process` and `osc_scope_resume` in the scope; a trap pins nothing: `Voice::new` only allocates, `attach` joins the phrase and takes the versions once the voice is boxed (the scope reserves its place before), its end gives the versions back before the mixes, a mix without memory plays directly, the store reserves the room of an entry before it takes a slot and never traps holding a buffer), `panic = "abort"`, fat LTO.
- The sound: a core computed every value as its TS one did, bit for bit (the same operations in the same order: no `mul_add`, no fast-math, no relaxed-simd: SIMD only over independent lanes, sums in their order, `as f32` where the TS wrote into a Float32Array, the Math of jsmath; "as the TS" in a comment of a crate names such an order), the goldens hold it since. Changed since, by explicit updates of the goldens: the voice played the two frames of a moving position in f32x4 (within 8.3·10⁻⁷ of before, 131–154 dB below the signal), then one frame at a time, discrete (the scenarios with a fractional or a moving position; the others bit for bit). The goldens: `<module>-goldens.spec.ts` renders the golden scenarios block by block (their hashes in `dsp/goldens/<module>.json`, `UPDATE_GOLDENS=1` records them again: a change of the sound is an explicit update) and random ones (`test-utils/fuzz-signals.ts`, `FUZZ_SCALE`; the nightly run × 250): finite, never over the ceiling of the limiter, every voice ending and giving its tables back. Node's own Math differs by machine (on arm64 its fdlibm is compiled with FMA contraction, its pow is the libm of the system), so the goldens specs import `test-utils/js-math.ts` first (Math becomes jsmath, the inputs and the levels the TS builds are the same everywhere; `**` runs as `Math.pow`: the babel plugin of `jest.config.ts`); `jsmath.spec.ts` checks jsmath against Node (x64: bit for bit, arm64: within 4 ulps) and against the hash of its outputs. The DSP specs of a module (`<module>-dsp.spec.ts`) run its core through its facade. The specs load `tmp/wasm-perf` (`test-utils/wasm.ts`, `wasm-transform.js` for the bytes a bundle imports): `npm run wasm` first.
- The wavetable oscillator (`dsp/wavetable-osc`: `envelope.rs`, `modulation.rs`, `filter.rs`, `store.rs`, `shared.rs`, `voice.rs`, `timeline.rs`, `scope.rs`; `wavetable-osc-wasm.ts`, `wavetable-osc.worklet.ts`): one instance per AudioWorkletGlobalScope holds the store, the shared and the voices (handles: `osc_store_new`, `osc_shared_new`, `osc_scope_new(rate)` the voices of a node with the ranges of the params, `osc_voice_new(rate, store, shared)` a voice alone, the facade the specs use; the specs make several stores and shareds in one instance). The scope (`scope.rs`, `createVoiceScopeWasm`): `osc_scope_on` (the options of a voice and its values in `osc_command`), `osc_scope_events` (the automation events of a message, 6 values each in `osc_command`), `osc_scope_routes`/`osc_scope_filter`, `osc_scope_bend` (the pitch bend of the node), `osc_scope_process(frame, frames)` (every voice: its 10 timelines or the arrays given by `osc_scope_direct`, the specs, then `Voice::process`, its output added to the sum at `osc_scope_output`), `osc_scope_fail` and `osc_scope_resume` (a voice that trapped ends, the block goes on with the next one; `fail` is false when the trap was not in a voice: the block is silence), `osc_scope_reports` (the starts and the ends since the last call), `osc_scope_tap`/`osc_scope_taps` (the output of every voice apart: the goldens of the scope, the parity of the browsers), `osc_scope_stats`, `osc_scope_free` (its voices end). The versions of a table live in the memory of the core (a slab, refcounted: `EntryRef`), a version no one holds gives its buffer to a pool (2 at most, the samples of one table of 256 frames at most): the smallest buffer of it that holds a table takes it, none does: the pool is freed before one is allocated (two sizes in turn: the memory stops growing after the first switches; a table that grows a frame at a time to 256 takes 239 MiB, two versions of up to 63.6 MiB live while the next comes and what dlmalloc can't join), the placeholder sine is the store's. A table arrives in chunks: the wasm store posts `{ chunks: STORE_CHUNK }` (1 MB) to the worker once it has its port, the builder (`wavetable-builder.ts`) then sends `begin` (the layout: `osc_store_begin` only reserves the room of the version) and the data chunk by chunk (`osc_store_chunk` gives the room of each in order and within the length of the version, zeroing only the chunk: the begin of a table of 256 frames 19 µs, it zeroed 64 MB on the audio thread; `commit` refuses a version a chunk of which is missing), the next once the store acked the last with its buffer (`createStoreReceiver`: a chunk is acked whatever happens to it, one that fails aborts its version, `osc_store_abort`, refused; the worker sends `{abort}` for a version it stopped sending, the port of a new worker aborts every slot and the receiver of the store processor before it drops what still comes, `epoch`), then frees its levels at once (`transfer(0)` detaches their buffer: kept for the next build, or left to a GC of the idle worker, a table of 256 frames stayed in it next to its copy in the core, S5 +68 MB; Safari before 17.4 leaves it to the GC), and the store replies nothing freed; a version the core has no memory for is `refused` (`try_reserve`), the latest one plays on. A version posted whole to its node (the offline engine of the specs, bench-osc) is copied in at once. The options of a voice (table ids interned per scope, the 8 random shares of each oscillator drawn by the facade in the order of the TS, then its `UnisonShape` and, for a random detune, 8 more), its routes (param names as ids) and its filter go through `osc_command`, the 10 params of a block through `osc_io` (one value or `frames`), the outputs through `osc_io_output`. `voices-processor.ts` is the voices processor of the worklet: the events of `processorOptions.events` before its first block, those of its port in order, a report after a block that has one; `tap` puts voice k in channels 2k and 2k + 1. The render loops wrap the phase in a cold call (else LLVM makes the wrap a select on the chain of the phase, a third slower than V8's code of the TS) and two neighbouring unison voices that both play their mixes render in one loop (`render11_pair`; their builds are decided first, in their order, so the budget and the counters stay those of the TS); frames are read through windows checked once per span, a mix is built over slices checked once (a check per read made the Lagrange upsampling of the short levels a quarter slower). Specs: `wavetable-osc-dsp.spec` and `wavetable-osc-budget.spec` (`test-utils/voice-dsp.ts`: a scope of voices of one instance of the core per spec file), `wavetable-osc-goldens.spec` (scopes of voices on a store and a shared, the envelopes curved as the app gives them; each scenario again as the voices of one node, the params as the arrays of each block through `osc_scope_direct`, every voice tapped: the same hash, and the sum of a block the f32 sum of the voices in their order, its own golden `<scenario>: the sum of one node`; random scopes both ways, and with a voice that traps), `wavetable-osc-scope.spec` (the timelines against the formulas of the spec through a voice, bit for bit; the order, the sum, the reports, a trap), `wavetable-osc.worklet.spec`, `wavetable-memory.spec` and the chunks of `wavetable-builder.spec`.
- The distortion core oversamples the hard clip 8× (the TS core did 4×: level B of the plan): a chain of its own (`StereoOversampler8`, `UP8_PHASES`/`DOWN8_PHASES` of `consts.rs`: `fir.sincPhase` at 8×, 32 taps per phase at the input rate as the 4× one, so the latency stays 32 samples), the soft clip stays on the 4× chain. A fade between modes on two chains runs both, each with its curve and its gains, their outputs added at the input rate; a chain that starts takes the input history of the other (its interpolator as if it had run, its decimator at 0: its points weighed 0 before), one that stops flushes its decimator with points of 0 for 33 samples, so the output is the one of both chains running all the time (the spec of the crate checks it). Hard clip of 7 kHz at 12 dB: aliases at −62.8 dB (4×: −50.2), 10 kHz −56.7 (−44.5); soft clip unchanged.
- A core: `fx_new(rate) → handle`, `fx_buffers(handle, frames)` (inL, inR, outL, outR in its memory, grown before the block), `fx_process(handle)`, `fx_clear(handle)` (forgets the sound before: a `{ clear }` message of `registerStereoProcessor`), `fx_free(handle)` (`stereo-processor.ts` frees it on `stop`); `stereoCore` keeps Float32Array views on the memory, made again when it grew (`createView` of `wasm-core.ts`; a pointer comes as an i32 and is read unsigned: past 2 GiB it is negative). The options of `fx_new` and the messages go through `fx_command` (a Float64Array in its memory): the reverb's `fx_set(handle, routed)` reads a count and key/value pairs (one `set` of the TS: the line scale follows once), the distortion's a bitmask of the keys present and the params; `fx_set_active`, `fx_idle`. Allocations only in constructors and when a buffer grows, never in a block. The functions of a core compile lazily, on their first call (the first block of a voice 0.93 ms in Node, then 0.014): each worklet module renders a few blocks of its own dsp while it loads and frees it (`prewarmStereo` of an impulse, over the ceiling of the limiter; the distortion both curves; the oscillator a voice of both oscillators on the placeholder sine, with a store and a shared of its own, a moving position, then a steady one), so neither the constructor nor the first block of a processor compiles them (the modules load with the app; the oscillator's prewarm takes 2 ms, before the first note). The distortion and the reverb take NaN and the infinities as 0 (`finite_inputs` of the stereo io before a block, a finite input to the bit; one silenced the distortion for good and the reverb until it was off), a block whose peak isn't a float clears the state and is silence; `wav.ts`, the decoded files and the worker take such samples as 0 too (the native delay holds a NaN in its loop in Chromium and WebKit). The fuzzers of the effects keep to what the app sends them (NaN, the infinities and 1e30 in their input too, the params in their ranges, beyond the top only where the core stays finite: a decay or a bass beyond its range blows the loops up, the knobs and the modulation never go there).

### Snapshots

`app/plugins/snapshot.ts` creates one `searchParamsSnapshotPlugin`. Models that register it (`osc1`, `osc2`, `filter`, `env`, `mod`, `fx`, synth as `v`) expose their state as `{ [modelName]: { key: value } }`:
- `getSnapshot()` / `applySnapshot(obj)` read and apply the state at runtime (used by presets); `$snapshotChanges` emits on any change.
- Legacy URL params `<modelName>-<key>=<json>` are still applied on init.
- `applySnap` must handle falsy values (`!== undefined`, not `&&`), and missing keys mean defaults.
- When adding persisted state, extend the model's `modelToSnap`/`applySnap` **and** `presetDataSchema` in `libs/preset-schema` (unknown keys are stripped, so a missing schema field is silently not saved).
- Oscillator keys: `w` is the Wave position in the frames played (fractional; of a table with a morph mark among its 256: the presets saved before discrete frames had it in the frames of the table: an update of the database recalculated them, frame j of a table with a morph mark to `keyFrame(j)`, a position past the end of the table to its last frame), `ph`/`pr` Phase and Phase rand (0..1, default `DEFAULT_PHASE` 0.5 = 180° and 1). A legacy `r` (Phase rand as a start delay of 0..2 ms) is read only: without `pr` it becomes `pr = min(1, r / 0.002)` (`withLegacyPhase`, in the schema, `applySnap` calls it for legacy urls) and is not written; such a legacy preset or url without `ph` gets `LEGACY_PHASE` 0 (it played from the start of the wave), not the default.
- Voicing `v`: `leg`, `port`, `max` and the range of the pitch bend down and up, `bd`/`bu` (semitones 0..48, 2 by default).

### Presets (apps/waveform/src/app/presets, netlify/functions)

- `libs/preset-schema`: zod schema of preset data with ranges and defaults, `normalizePreset` (4 significant digits, sorted keys), `presetHash` (sha256 of normalized JSON, name excluded → dedup), profanity filter. Shared by the client and functions (functions import it by relative path, esbuild doesn't know tsconfig aliases).
- API (Netlify Functions v2, routed by `config.path`): `presets.mts` handles `/api/presets` (list/search with keyset cursors, save with Turnstile + 5/hour/IP), `/api/presets/top`, `/api/presets/:id` (GET, and PUT to overwrite), `/api/presets/:id/report`, `/api/presets/:id/opened`, `/api/presets/name-available?name=`; `usage.mts` stores listens and stays (each with a Turnstile token of its own). IPs are stored only as salted hashes.
- Scheduled: `recompute-scores.mts` (every 30 min) and `cleanup.mts` (daily, removes unqualified presets 90 days after they were saved, edited or opened by their author: `GREATEST(created_at, updated_at, author_opened_at)`; listeners not heard for 60 days; reports of hidden presets and those older than 180 days, so REPORT_HIDE_THRESHOLD counts the reports of half a year).
- Scores (`recompute-scores`, last 60 days): a listener is an `ip_hash` (`getIpHash` hashes `ipKey`: IPv4 as is, IPv6 by its /64, privacy addresses rotate), not a client id (clearing localStorage would make new ones). A listen is playing a chosen preset `USAGE_LISTEN_SECONDS` (10), a stay `USAGE_STAY_SECONDS` (60, both `libs/preset-schema`), both without switching to another one; presets skipped sooner are not counted (the top is stepped through with ◀ ▶ the most, their listens would weigh against it). The client sends each once a preset is opened (`POST /api/usage` `{ presetId, event: 'listen' | 'stay', turnstileToken }`), `usage.mts` keeps one `listens` row (migration 004) per preset and IP with the days of the last listen (`heard`) and stay (`stayed`, NULL if none), each renewed at most once a day; a stay sets `heard` too, so `stayed <= heard`. Listens on hidden presets and on the author's own ones (the preset's `ip_hash`) are not stored, the preset opened on start is not counted at all (its listeners didn't choose it). Each listen and stay weighs w, a 7-day half-life since its day. `score` = the share of listeners who stayed with a Bayesian prior: (Σ w stays + k·m) / (Σ w listens + k), m the site's share: Σ over all presets of their shares, each weighing min(Σ w listens, k), k = SCORE_PRIOR_LISTENERS (with every listen counted the top would make the average itself and its lower part would keep falling below it, out of the top); a share, not a count, so the top's extra traffic (start, ◀ ▶) gives it no extra score, and a preset with few listeners stays near m. `unique_users` = the listeners who stayed (shown in the lists). Only presets with MIN_UNIQUE_USERS of them get a score (and `qualified_at`). `cleanup` removes rows not heard for 60 days.
- Popular: `preset_scores.popular` with POPULAR_MIN_USERS listeners who stayed and a share not below m (m is a rounded numeric quotient, compared with a 1e-12 tolerance, so exactly the average counts): the bar doesn't grow with the audience, a preset found in the list gets in with listeners who stay. `/api/presets/top` (Popular tab, ◀ ▶, 50 presets) has every featured preset first (curated, `presets.featured`, whatever its score), the rest of it are such presets by score; the `popular` sort of the list orders all presets by score, whatever the threshold.
- Client: `presets` rxModel (current preset, dirty flag via hash comparison, ◀ ▶ list, `?preset=<id>` loading; a preset and Init are applied through the sound switch, its `setCurrent` too (a preset that throws as it is applied: a toast, it doesn't become current), the restore of the tab at once; specs without the synth pass `immediateSoundSwitch` of test-utils), `usePresetUsageTracker` (counts the seconds an opened preset is played: audio runs, tab is visible and a note is held or was played or released in the last 30 s; once a preset reaches `USAGE_LISTEN_SECONDS` / `USAGE_STAY_SECONDS` without switching it reports the listen / the stay, each once; the `auto` preset is not counted until it is chosen; another preset drops a listen or stay not reached, a hidden tab only pauses it), `UsageReporter` (rendered by `PresetSection`) sends them one at a time, each through a Turnstile widget of its own (`appearance: 'interaction-only'`, out of the layout, the script loads with the first event): the visitor never sees it, an event Cloudflare would ask to interact for, a failed check or no token in `CAPTCHA_TIMEOUT_MS` (30 s) is dropped.
- Start without `?preset=`: `openPopular` opens a random one of `/api/presets/top` as `auto` (unless something was loaded, saved or changed meanwhile: the state must still hash to `presetHash({})`). Random, not the first: no single preset is opened for every visitor; ▶ continues from it. An `auto` preset is not put in the url (after a reload it would open from the link as chosen). Init (the button between the name and ▶, `reset`) applies `normalizePreset({})` (not `applySnapshot({})`: some `applySnap` keep missing keys), clears the preset and the url. The tab keeps what a reload would lose (sessionStorage `presetState`: the current preset, its loaded hash and the params, written 200 ms after a change and on `pagehide`): an edited preset, the `auto` one, an edited Init; not a clean preset (its link opens it) nor Init itself (the start opens a popular one). On start it is applied (through `normalizePreset`) instead of loading when its preset is the one of the url (none for `auto` and Init), so a link to another preset in the same tab opens that one. UI: `PresetPanel` in the header, `PresetBrowser` and `SaveDialog` antd modals. Cmd/Ctrl+S opens the save dialog.
- Editing: on create the server returns a secret `editToken` (only its sha256 is stored in `presets.edit_token_hash`); the client keeps tokens in `localStorage.presetEditTokens`. `PUT /api/presets/:id` with the token overwrites sound and name (no Turnstile, 1 update per 5 s, 409 if the sound is another preset's). Presets created before migration 002 have no token and can't be edited. Opening an own preset (`applyPreset` with a token in `localStorage`, once a session) posts the token to `POST /api/presets/:id/opened`, which sets `presets.author_opened_at` (migration 005, at most once a day, a wrong token changes nothing): the author keeps it from the cleanup.
- Names are unique case-insensitively with collapsed whitespace (`presetNameSchema` normalizes, `presets_name_key` is a unique index on `lower(name)`, migration 003). Hidden presets keep their names. The save dialog checks availability while typing; saving the same sound under a taken name still returns the existing preset (dedup by hash comes first).
- QWERTY notes are ignored while focus is in a text field (`keyboard-controller.ts`); a keyup releases only the note its key pressed, whatever the modifiers (not one of MIDI or the piano), and all of them are released when Cmd is (macOS sends no keyup of a key let go while Cmd is held) or the window loses focus (`$blur` of the app model). The on-screen piano releases a key on blur too, and a pointer that moves without a button (its pointerup lost).

### Wavetables

- The Netlify functions (`netlify/functions/get-wavetables-list.ts`, `get-wavetable.ts`) list the wavetable files in an S3 bucket as a folder tree and serve them. The client fetches the list with react-query (`synth/oscillator/hooks/use-wavetables.ts`). The table itself is loaded by the oscillator model from `$waveTablePath` (last path element = S3 key, `synth/services/wavetable.ts`, cached per session, 2 retries), so it follows presets whatever screen is shown; `WaveSelector` only shows `$waveTableLoading`.
- The files are wavetables in mono 32-bit float WAV (44.1 kHz, 2048-sample waves, 256 at most: the manual editor adds no wave to a table of 256), most of them with a mark (the `clm ` chunk, `<!>2048 10000000 …`: the samples of a frame, then flags). A mark whose first flag is not 0 is a morph: the table plays expanded to 256 frames by crossfades, whatever that flag: frame j of N at round(j·255/(N−1)), between two the next one weighs (i + 1)/(s + 1) at i frames into a span of s (`wavetable-frames.ts`, checked against `dsp/measurements/frames.json`; the second flag changes nothing it plays). `synth/services/wav.ts` reads their samples and the mark from the bytes (`loadWavetable` gives `{ frames, morph }`); not `decodeAudioData`, which resamples to the context rate (at 48 kHz a 2048-sample slice is 1.09 cycles and a table has ~278 waves instead of 256).
- The wavetable editor (`synth/wave-table-editor/`) also builds tables from manual drawing or from an uploaded audio file. `synth/services/audio-file.ts` reads that file at its own rate, never at the context's: a WAV through `wav.ts`, other formats decoded by an `OfflineAudioContext` at 44.1 kHz (their rate is unknown before decoding).

### Libs

Libs are imported through the path aliases in `tsconfig.base.json`: `@waveform/math`, `@waveform/rxjs-react`, `@waveform/ui-kit` and `@waveform/preset-schema`.

- `math`: number, vector and typed-array helpers, plus FFT-based wave utilities.
- `ui-kit`: styled-components UI widgets (charts, knobs/handles, piano, the pitch wheel, analysers, ADSR) and `theme`/`GlobalStyle`. `Handle` and `Fader` take `name` (the full name of what the knob changes, 1–2 short words, before the value in the tooltip while it is held, `ValueTooltip`; a tooltip that doesn't fit the width of the viewport on one line, a knob at its edge, stacks it over the value (`NARROW_ATTR` of `Tooltip`, until it hides) and, if it still doesn't, moves inside it, 4 px from the edge; a string `label` by default; the fader's accessible name: the FX knobs have it in their `FxParamDef`), `mode` linear, log or pow (`valueScale`), `modulations` (only the `selected` one shows an arc of the color of its source over the whole ring of the knob or a bar as wide as the slot beside it; a control with routes has an `AmountDot` (`RoutesDot`), 14 px, an equal section of the color of each route clockwise from the top (an SVG of `arc`s: jsdom drops a conic-gradient) with a sine over them (the sign of a modulation), stuck to the outer edge of the knob's ring at the top left (above the top left of a fader), centered on the point of its style, a hit area of 20 px: with a `selected` route drag to change it, double click or long press to remove it; without one it only shows the routes, a press on it goes to the control (`passive`; a fader drags from it relative to its value, as from its cap); `useModMark`: a mark like the knob's or the fader's value line, at 0.5 opacity, where the sum of amount × level of all the live routes moves it, drawn every frame without a render; `useLiveDraw(knobs, draw, active)` draws a chart of knobs the same way: at once, again when its knobs or `draw` change, and in every frame the routes moved a knob in, at most every `MIN_FRAME_MS` (12 ms: every other frame at 120 Hz), at the values they move them to (the steps of the mark, `routedShare` of `mod-routes.ts`); its paths have no `d` in React, only the drawing sets it (`useLivePath`: one writer, a `d` that didn't change is not written, the browser would restyle, lay out and paint the path again), from typed arrays (`responseInto`, `transferCurve`, `decayCurve`, `periodPoints`: the lowest and the highest sample of each pixel column past 2 per column) through `linePath`/`areaPath` (0.1 px, the d3 scales as `affine` k·v + b, written as ASCII bytes decoded once: no call and no string per number); its mark (a dot at its edge, 2 px, 3 px on a large knob) shows the value: a knob that takes modulations (it gets a `modulations` array, maybe empty) has a translucent grey ring of 6 px on its edge for its travel, one that takes none a grey arc of 2 px for its travel 2 px out of the knob (`sizes[size].arc`, under the knob's shadow) with a green fill on it from fillFrom (min by default) to the value (the fader has a scale, `scaleTicks`: dots every 10%, short lines at 0, 50, 100% and fillFrom) and `modTarget` (the drop target of `startModDrag`, none while disabled). The app also uses antd 5, themed in `app/index.tsx`.
